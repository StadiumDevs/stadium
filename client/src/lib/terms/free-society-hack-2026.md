## Scope

These terms cover every project submitted to Free Society Hack in Mumbai (hack Wed 4 Nov 2026, demo day Thu 5 Nov 2026), on both tracks: Privacy (Zcash) and Agentic Commerce (by Bitrefill). You accept them by ticking **"I agree to the terms of the hack"** when you submit on Stadium. Sections on prizes only matter if your project wins.

Free Society Hack is run by WebZero, with Privacy track prizes funded by Zcash Community Grants (ZCG). Bitrefill runs, judges and pays its own track.

## Who can submit

1. You must be an approved Luma guest who checked in at the venue on Wed 4 Nov: True Tramm Trunk BKC, G2-B, Ground Floor, Trade Centre BKC, opposite MTNL, G Block, Bandra Kurla Complex, Bandra East, Mumbai 400098 (on Luma)
2. You can build solo or with others. Each project names **one point person**, who submits, is accountable for the project, and receives any prize. There is no team prize; splitting it is up to you.
3. Organisers, judges and anyone who scores a track cannot win a prize on that track.

## Submitting

Submit on Stadium by **Thu 5 Nov, 00:00 IST**. Late or edited-after-deadline submissions are not judged. Pick your track, or both, and fill in everything that track requires.

| Track | Required | Optional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Privacy (Zcash) | One-line pitch · one testnet transaction ID your project created · public repo with a README listing every technology used · privacy and trust note · your next step for the 30 days · public URL, if it is an app people use | Demo video (2 min max, preferred) · friction notes |
| Agentic Commerce (Bitrefill) | Public repo · demo video (3 min max) or live link showing the agent make a purchase · Bitrefill feedback form | None |

By submitting you confirm that:

1. The work is yours and your team's, and you have the right to submit it, including any third-party code under its licence.
2. Any work started before the hack (for example at Zecathon) is disclosed. Only what was built or shown working at the hack is scored.
3. Privacy track projects run on testnet only, with no mainnet funds. Agentic Commerce projects use test products and test access, with no real money spent.
4. Nothing in the submission is faked or mocked without saying so.

## Your code and your project

1. You keep ownership of everything you build.
2. You allow WebZero, ZCG, Zcash ecosystem partners and Bitrefill to show your project's name, pitch, demo video, screenshots and the point person's name in event recaps, reports and social posts.
3. Your submission, judge notes and friction notes are shared with ZCG as part of the grant report. Friction notes are passed to the teams who can fix the issues.

## Judging

1. Privacy track: five judges score in Stadium on Works, Private, Easy, Needed and Continuing (0–3 each) plus a Bonus (0 or 1), out of 16. Scores are averaged across the judges who scored the project; a project whose average Works score is 0 cannot win. Ties go to the higher Continuing score, then Needed.
2. Agentic Commerce track: three Bitrefill judges score out of 5 on use of the Bitrefill stack, following the requirements, and innovation.
3. A project can enter and win on both tracks.
4. Winners are announced on stage at demo day, Thu 5 Nov. You don't have to demo to win.
5. Judges' and organisers' decisions are final.

A project can be disqualified, before or after prizes are announced, if it hides prior work, fakes a core step without saying so, uses someone else's work as its own, uses mainnet funds or real money, or if its point person breaks the code of conduct. A disqualified project forfeits any prize, and any prize already paid must be returned.

### How you get paid

1. **After demo day,** we email each winning Privacy point person a payout form. Send it back within 14 days, by Thu 19 Nov.
2. **The form asks for** your legal name, country of tax residence, your USDC wallet address and the network it is on, and, if required, a government ID and PAN (for Indian residents).
3. **We pay** within 14 days of receiving complete details. You get an email with the transaction link when the payment is sent. Double-check your address: a payment sent to the address you gave can't be recovered.
4. **No response by Thu 19 Nov?** We follow up once. A prize not claimed by Fri 4 Dec is forfeited and returned to the programme.

### The Ecosystem Prize

Judges name two Ecosystem Prize winners from the five Privacy podium projects. Each is matched with an ecosystem partner and receives a further USD 100 on completing the 30 days:

1. **Agree** a one-sentence milestone with your partner by Fri 13 Nov.
2. **Deliver** it with a public link by Fri 4 Dec; your partner confirms it is done.
3. **Share** it at a ZecHub Friday Lounge by Fri 4 Dec.

The USD 100 is paid on Fri 11 Dec to the same payout details. A milestone is a real next step for you, about ten hours of public work, usually on your own project. If you don't complete, you keep your day prize; there is no other penalty. The organisers' decision on completion is final.

### Things to know

- **Taxes are yours.** Prizes are paid in full, unless the law requires us to withhold tax. You are responsible for declaring and paying any tax due where you live.
- **Fees:** we cover the network fee for sending USDC. Fees for moving or converting it afterwards are yours.
- **Paid only to the point person,** in their own name. We don't split payments between team members or pay third parties.
- **Bitrefill gift cards** are sent by Bitrefill, who contacts each winning point person by email after demo day. Questions about them go to Bitrefill.
- We will never ask for your seed phrase, private keys or passwords.
