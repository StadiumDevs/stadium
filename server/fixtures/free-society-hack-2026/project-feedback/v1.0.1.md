## Scope

All 48 Privacy track submissions (Privacy only and both tracks), minus the Example Project. Agentic Commerce-only submissions have no scores in this export and are not covered.

## Method

- Scores are the judge-offset normalized averages from Stadium. "Strongest" and "weakest" compare each criterion with the track median for that criterion, so they show where a project stood out, not just its highest raw number.
- Judges' notes are anonymised: no judge is named, and notes are paraphrased rather than quoted. Vague notes (for example a single word) are left out.
- Every factual claim in a judge's note was checked before use. Claims that could be checked are listed under fact checks with their source. Claims that couldn't be checked are either dropped or worded as what a judge observed during judging.
- Projects with no judge notes get feedback from their scores only, marked basis = scores.

Track medians (normalized, out of 3): Works 2.19, Private 1.72, Easy 1.57, Needed 1.31, Continuing 1.58.
